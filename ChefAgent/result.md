With leftover chicken and rice, you have the base for a quick and delicious **15-Minute Chicken Fried Rice**. 

*(Note: If you are scaling this, standard proportions use about **2 cups of cooked rice** for every **1 cup of diced chicken**).*

Here is how to make it in a few simple steps:

### 1. Prep Your Ingredients
* 2 cups cooked leftover rice (cold is best!)
* 1 to 1.5 cups diced or shredded leftover chicken
* 1 egg (beaten)
* 2 tablespoons soy sauce
* 1 teaspoon sesame oil
* *Optional add-ins:* A handful of frozen peas/carrots, chopped green onions, or minced garlic if you have them.

### 2. Sauté the Base
* Heat a skillet or pan over medium-high heat with a splash of cooking oil.
* If using veggies or garlic, toss them in first and stir-fry for 1-2 minutes until tender.

### 3. Add Chicken and Rice
* Add your leftover chicken and cold rice to the pan.
* Break up any clumps of rice with a spatula.
* Stir-fry everything together for 3–4 minutes until thoroughly heated through.

### 4. Scramble the Egg
* Push the rice and chicken mixture to one side of the pan, clearing a small spot.
* Pour the beaten egg into the empty space.
* Let it cook for 30 seconds, then scramble it with your spatula before mixing it into the rice.

### 5. Season and Serve
* Drizzle the soy sauce and sesame oil over the top.
* Toss everything together for 1 more minute until fragrant and well combined. 
* Serve hot and enjoy!
